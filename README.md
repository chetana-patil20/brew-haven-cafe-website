# Brew Haven Cafe — Official Website

A responsive, modern cafe website built with semantic **HTML5**, **CSS3**, and **Vanilla JavaScript** for **Brew Haven Cafe** in Kalaburagi, Karnataka.

## Included Features
- **Brand Identity & Hero Section:** Showcases Brew Haven Cafe's 4.3★ Google rating (38 reviews), ₹200–400 per person price range, Dine-in & Takeaway options, and direct Google Maps & Instagram links.
- **Interactive Filterable Menu:** Highlights signature favorites (**Blue Ocean Cooler**, **French Fries**) with category filters and real-time search.
- **Visual Gallery & Ambience:** Dedicated photo showcase highlighting food, coolers, and birthday/hangout vibes.
- **Customer Reviews Showcase:** Features authentic Google review highlights and direct link to read all reviews.
- **Location, Hours & Embedded Map:** Includes the full AK Complex, Sedam Road address, Plus Code (`8VF7+MH Kalaburagi`), one-click address copy, and an interactive Google Maps embed.
- **Table & Birthday Booking Modal:** Lets guests compose a table inquiry that automatically copies to their clipboard and opens `@brew_haven_cafe23` on Instagram.
